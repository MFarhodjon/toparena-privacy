TopArena Account Deletion

Last updated: February 26, 2026

This page explains how to initiate account deletion and what data is removed.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

How to Request Deletion

  1. Open Profile > Settings > Delete Account in the app.
  2. Review the deletion warning and confirm the request.
  3. If you cannot access your account, contact toparena.app@gmail.com to recover access, then complete in-app deletion.

App Review note: Account deletion initiation is available in-app at Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

What Is Deleted

  • Account profile, authentication linkage, and personal profile media.
  • User-owned content and activity where removal is technically possible without breaking league history integrity.

What May Be Retained

  • Security logs and limited records required for fraud prevention or legal compliance.
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics that cannot identify you.

Deletion Timeline

Requests are acknowledged within 7 days and finalized within 30 days, unless legal obligations require longer retention for specific records.

Confirmation and Safety

TopArena may ask for account ownership verification before irreversible deletion to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.
