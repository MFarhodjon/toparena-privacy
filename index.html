TopArena Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Contact: toparena.app@gmail.com

Terms of Use Account Deletion

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, and authentication identifier.
  • Profile data: optional profile photo and optional team details.
  • App activity: teams, leagues, matches, and settings you create or update.
  • Support data: information you share when contacting support.

2. How We Use Data

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Let you join and manage teams, leagues, and matches.
  • Show profile and team information inside the app.
  • Provide support and improve stability/security.

3. Permissions

TopArena asks for photo library access only when you choose to upload media, such as a profile photo or team logo.

4. Storage and Sharing

TopArena uses Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) to run app features. We do not sell personal data. Limited profile or team information may be visible to other users according to app features.

5. Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. After confirmed account deletion, data is deleted or anonymized, except records that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and update profile data.
  • Request account deletion.

Deletion details: Account Deletion.

7. Children

TopArena is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are posted on this page with the latest date.
