TopArena Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

This page describes how TopArena collects, uses, stores, discloses, and deletes personal data when you use the TopArena mobile app.

Contact: toparena.app@gmail.com

Terms of Use Account Deletion

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, and encrypted authentication identifiers.
  • Profile data: optional profile photo and optional team details.
  • App activity data: teams, leagues, matches, and settings created or updated in the app.
  • Support data: information you provide when contacting support.

TopArena does not request government IDs, precise GPS location, or payment card details in the app.

2. Why We Use Data

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Let you join, manage, and view teams, leagues, and matches.
  • Show your profile and related content to other users according to app features.
  • Provide support, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Legal Bases and Consent

  • We process data to provide the service you request (account, team, and match features).
  • Where required by law or platform rules, we request your consent before data collection or access to protected resources.
  • You can withdraw optional permissions (for example, photo access) in iOS Settings at any time.

4. Permissions and Purpose Strings (iOS)

TopArena requests photo library access only if you choose to upload media.

  • Main use case: choose a profile photo.
  • Additional use case: choose a team logo or related image for team and league pages.

Recommended iOS purpose string:

TopArena uses your photo library so you can select a profile photo or team logo that appears in your account and league pages.

If the app saves images to Photos, provide a separate and specific NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription string.

5. Tracking and Advertising Identifiers

TopArena does not track users across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes by default.

If tracking is enabled in a future version, TopArena will request permission through Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) before tracking and before accessing IDFA.

6. Storage, Security, and Third-Party Processors

TopArena uses Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage) to operate account and app features.

  • Data is protected in transit and at rest using industry-standard safeguards.
  • Third-party service providers that process TopArena user data are required to provide the same or equal level of privacy protection described in this policy.

7. Sharing

TopArena does not sell personal data. Limited profile/team information may be visible to other users where required by app features.

TopArena may disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect users, platform security, or legal rights.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained while your account is active. After confirmed account deletion, personal data is deleted or anonymized, except data that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Deletion details and request steps are available at Account Deletion.

9. User Rights and Privacy Choices

  • Access and update profile data.
  • Request export of your personal data.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data.

You can submit requests at Account Deletion or by email: toparena.app@gmail.com.

11. Children

TopArena is not directed to children under 13, and TopArena does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

12. International Transfers

Because TopArena uses cloud infrastructure providers, your data may be processed in jurisdictions outside your country, with reasonable safeguards in place.

13. Policy Availability and Updates

This Privacy Policy is available in the app and through App Store metadata links. We publish policy updates on this page with the updated date.

Compliance note: This policy is written to align with Apple App Store privacy requirements, including data collection clarity, third-party safeguards, retention/deletion transparency, and user deletion rights.
